# Deck 25 · ໝໍຜີ ອາຈານແດງ (Mo Phi · Ajarn Daeng)

Approved by the owner on 2026-10-31. DECKS index 24, deck number 25 (append at the end of `DECKS`).

## Idea
A cool young Lao spirit doctor who makes friends with spirits. He never destroys: he calls kind spirits to help and
**sends troublesome cards home** (back to the owner's hand). Kid-friendly: spirits are cute glowing wisps and kind
folk spirits, no scary faces. Style tags for the deck: `ຍັນ ✴, ສົ່ງກັບບ້ານ, ຜີໃຈດີ, ນາງຕານີ ປ່ຽນຮ່າງ`.

Colors: deep indigo night, glowing gold yantra lines, soft teal spirit light, moonlight silver, red accents.

## New tribe
`mophi` · `ເຜົ່າໝໍຜີ` · color deep red-gold (`#d8452c`) · particles: gold yantra sparks · symbol ✴.
The deck also uses the existing `ghost` tribe (deck 2), so the two mix in the deck builder.

## Deck meter: ຍັນ ✴ (mech `yan`, 0/5)
- +1 line each time you play a `mophi` card or a spell.
- At 5: **ຍັນເຕັມ!** a gold yantra flashes on your side (a disc under it, no full-screen overlay):
  your characters +1 power this turn, summon 1 ຜີນ້ອຍໂຄມໄຟ, then the meter goes back to 0.
- Cards that care about a full yantra: ກຸມານທອງ, ໝໍສັກຍັນ, ນາງຕານີ ຮ່າງແສງເດືອນ, ເປົ່າຄາຖາ, ອາຈານແດງ.

## Nang Tani's change (the deck's star)
- ນາງຕານີ enters → plants ຕົ້ນກ້ວຍເງິນ (token, power 0, `hide`: can't be attacked, spells can hit it), countdown 3.
- Each of your turn starts: countdown −1 (−2 while ອາຈານແດງ is on your field).
- At 0, if ນາງຕານີ is still on your field: the tree is used up and ນາງຕານີ becomes **ນາງຕານີ ຮ່າງແສງເດືອນ**
  (keeps items, loses damage marks), with a big entrance like a legend (banana leaves swirl, full moon).
- If ນາງຕານີ is gone, the tree just waits (it changes the next ນາງຕານີ that enters straight away).
- Counterplay: destroy the tree with a spell or destroy ນາງຕານີ before the count ends.

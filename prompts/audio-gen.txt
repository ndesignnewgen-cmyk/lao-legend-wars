LAO LEGEND WARS · MUSIC AND SOUND EFFECTS, ROUND 2 (8 music tracks + 33 sound effects)
These replace the sounds the game now builds in code. The code sounds stay as a backup for anything you don't send.

=====================================================================================================
WHERE TO MAKE THEM
=====================================================================================================
MUSIC → Suno (suno.com). Create → turn on "Custom" → turn on "Instrumental" →
        paste STYLE into "Style of Music" → open "More Options" and paste EXCLUDE into "Exclude styles" →
        leave Lyrics empty → Title = the file name (menu, battle …) → Create.
        Each Create gives 2 songs. Listen and keep the one that sounds most Lao (clear khaen and phin) with a steady beat.
        Download: tap ⋯ on the song → Download → MP3 Audio.
        Licence: songs made on the free plan are for non-commercial use only. If the game will ever earn money
        (ads, shop, paid packs), make the final versions while on a paid plan.
        Songs come out 2–4 minutes long. That's fine: I cut the loop and the part the game needs myself.

SOUND EFFECTS → ElevenLabs Sound Effects (elevenlabs.io → Sound Effects). Paste the PROMPT, then open the settings:
        Duration = the seconds given, Prompt influence = high (about 0.7). Generate, listen to the takes, download the best as MP3.
        Free plan: non-commercial and you must credit ElevenLabs. A paid plan removes both.
        Free with no conditions: Pixabay Sound Effects (pixabay.com/sound-effects) or Kenney.nl (CC0). Search the
        "SEARCH" words given under each sound.

NAMES AND SENDING
· Save each file with exactly the name in [brackets] (menu.mp3, hit.mp3 …). mp3, wav or m4a are all fine.
· Send two zip files: music.zip and sfx.zip. If you can't decide between takes, send both (hit-a.mp3, hit-b.mp3).
· Volume doesn't matter: the game evens it out.

MUSIC (8)
=====================================================================================================
For every track: instrumental only, no singing, no humming, no spoken words. Lao instruments: khaen (bamboo mouth organ),
phin (plucked Lao lute), pong lang / khong wong (wooden xylophone), ching (small finger cymbals), klong (barrel drum),
khong (big gong). A light orchestra under them is welcome. Bright, adventurous and family-friendly, like an animated adventure film.
Never scary, never sad. A steady beat the whole way through (I cut the loops on bar lines).

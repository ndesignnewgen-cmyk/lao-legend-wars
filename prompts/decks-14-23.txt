(132 card pictures + 20 epic alt arts + 10 deck boxes)

HOW TO MAKE THEM
Please create these images one at a time, in order, numbered. After each image, continue with the next one without asking me. If you have to stop, I will type 'continue' and you go on from the next number.
Save each image as its number only (1.png, 2.png …). The word in [brackets] at the start of each item is a file label for me: never draw it, never write any text in a picture.
For the same character, keep the same design in every image (face, colors, clothes). Each deck lists its recurring characters: keep them identical everywhere they appear.
Every picture is for children: friendly, brave and joyful, never scary, no blood, no weapons hurting anyone, no injuries, modest clothing, kind expressions even on big monsters.
All people are Lao: Lao faces and skin tones, Lao clothing (sinh skirts with woven hem patterns, pha biang shoulder sashes, pha khao ma checked cloth, salong), Lao houses on stilts, Lao temples with sweeping tiered roofs and naga roof ends, the Mekong river, sticky rice baskets (tip khao), kranok flame ornaments.
Card pictures: Portrait 63:88 (for example 1062x1482). Keep the main subject in the upper half of the picture and leave the bottom 40% as a simple, calm, softly lit background, because the card's text box covers it. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no frame, no border, no watermark.

COMMON CARD STYLE (each deck adds its own colors and mood):
Full-art trading card illustration in a Southeast Asian comic cartoon style: thick bold black outlines, bright clean colors with soft cel shading, big expressive eyes and funny friendly faces, rich detailed background, warm rim light around the subject, strong clear silhouette, clean readable composition, very high detail, polished like a premium collectible card game.

LEGEND CARDS (marked LEGEND): the most magnificent picture of the deck: heroic centered pose, glowing golden aura, light rays from behind, floating sparkles and kranok flame shapes in the light, richer detail than the other cards.

EPIC ALT ART STYLE (marked EPIC ALT ART): very detailed and dramatic painterly illustration, cinematic lighting, dynamic diagonal composition, rich textures, glowing magic particles, deep colors with bright highlights, not cartoon-simple, still suitable for children. Portrait 63:88, faces in the upper half, no text, no frame.

DECK BOX STYLE (the 10 box images at the end): a premium trading-card deck box standing upright on a pure white background, seen from slightly to the left so the front face and a narrow right side are visible, soft shadow under it. The box has a thick ornate gold Lao kranok frame around the front, a smooth blank shiny gold band across the top of the front, and a blank smooth gold ribbon banner across the lower part of the front (leave the band and ribbon completely empty: the game writes the deck name there). The deck's illustration fills the front between the band and the ribbon. The side panel has a repeating Lao woven pattern in the deck's colors. No text anywhere.

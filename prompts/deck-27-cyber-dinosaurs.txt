LAO LEGEND WARS · DECK 27 · LOST KINGDOM OF CYBER DINOSAURS

Create each requested asset as ONE SEPARATE image. Bracketed filenames are file labels only; do not draw them. Produce assets sequentially, keeping designs consistent. This is an original fantasy world, not a reconstruction of a real archaeological site.

SHARED ART DIRECTION
Premium, richly detailed, dimensional fantasy trading-card illustration with painterly rendering, tactile scales, convincing anatomy, sophisticated cinematic light and clear silhouettes. Majestic creatures with intelligence and personality. Suitable for a teen/adult folklore fantasy game. Not chibi, not toy-like, not a flat cartoon. No blood, gore, exposed organs, injury, horror faces, skeletons, people, text, letters, numerals, logos, watermarks, UI or card borders.
An imagined prehistoric karst valley inspired by Laos: massive limestone cliffs, broad-leaf jungle, cycads, waterfalls, giant ferns and invented ancient Lao-inspired stone gateways. Natural dinosaurs first; cyber forms add elegant protective technology to the SAME living creature. Visible healthy organic scales and eyes must remain. No surgery, body damage or exposed flesh. These are dinosaur-shaped cyborg guardians, never humanoid robots or piloted machines. Original designs, no copied film, game or toy creatures.
Palette: emerald, graphite, antique gold, soft ivory and restrained turquoise energy. Lao-inspired flame curls and woven geometric motifs engraved along armor edges, no writing. Gold chest crystals and turquoise circuit seams. Glows must not obscure anatomy.

CARD IMAGE FORMAT
Portrait 63:88. Strong subject in the upper 60%; bottom 40% is quiet, softly lit continuation of the scene, with no key face or essential detail there. No baked-in frame, stats or captions. When a creature is shown, prioritize a readable head and silhouette. Keep its limbs and tail coherent, not merged into foliage or armor.

LOCKED RECURRING DESIGNS
- KING REX / dinoking: massive living tyrannosaur, deep emerald scales, ivory throat, amber eyes, one distinctive antique-gold crescent-shaped brow marking and five small natural gold-edged scutes descending from the nape. Powerful hind legs, two small forearms, a long heavy balancing tail. No giant humanoid arms, no wings. Its royal marking distinguishes it from ordinary rex hatchlings.
- META REX / dinometarex: exactly the same KING REX head, brow marking, eyes, scale colors, body proportions and five nape scutes. Elegant graphite segmented armor with antique-gold Lao-inspired trim on skull, chest, spine, thighs and tail; clearly visible organic jaw, neck folds, flanks and toes between protective pieces. One warm-gold chest crystal, two low integrated shoulder energy vents, turquoise seams. Armor enhances its dinosaur silhouette. No giant weapons hiding its face, no cockpit.
- RAPTOR / dinoraptor: lean teal-blue predator, gold lateral stripe, small amber eyes, short feathering along forearms and tail, long balanced tail, raised sickle toe claws. Agile rather than monstrous.
- META RAPTOR / dinometaraptor: the identical RAPTOR with lightweight graphite/gold armor, narrow turquoise energy edges around its existing toe claws, small flank energy fins; original scales, stripe and feathers remain visible. No new humanoid limbs or giant wings.
- TRICERATOPS / dinotrike: stocky terracotta-brown quadruped, three ivory horns, broad scalloped frill with a pale gold sunburst pattern, calm determined eyes.
- META TRICERATOPS / dinometatrike: the identical TRICERATOPS with ivory/gold segmented armor around its frill and shoulders, reinforced caps on all three existing horns, a thin curved turquoise energy shield following its frill. Organic cheek, legs and belly visible.
- ORDINARY REX FAMILY: olive-green scales with cream spots, no royal gold brow mark. Hatchling and adult are the same species and share markings. Distinct from KING REX.
